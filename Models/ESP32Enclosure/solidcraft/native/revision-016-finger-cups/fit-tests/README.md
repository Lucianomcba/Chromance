# Finger cups: quick feel and print-finish samples

Print [finger-cup-samples.3mf](finger-cup-samples.3mf) before choosing the final
cup depth. These are four 3 mm thick caps extracted from the actual native
slider geometry, including the outer chamfer. They do not fit into the button
frames. For a working mechanism test, print the
[two replacement sliders](../replacement-sliders.3mf) instead.

Objects appear left to right along print-layout X:

| Object name | Cup depth | Cup orientation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cup_040_down` | 0.4 mm | Toward bed; production slider pose |
| `cup_060_down` | 0.6 mm | Toward bed; default |
| `cup_080_down` | 0.8 mm | Toward bed; stronger dish |
| `cup_060_up` | 0.6 mm | Upward; surface-finish comparison only |

All cups are 7 mm in diameter. [Preview](finger-cup-samples.png).

Use the intended slider material, nozzle, layer height and process settings.
The downward cups require bridging the shallow recess; initially inspect the
slicer preview without filling those cavities with support. Actual bridging,
first-layer adhesion and feel need checking on your printer. The upward sample
is a comparison, not approval to flip the complete slider without reviewing its
other overhangs, arm strength and support needs.

Check that the rim and cup feel comfortable under a fingertip, with no sharp or
rough bridge strands. Compare 0.4/0.6/0.8 mm and record the preferred depth. Do
not sand guide surfaces to fix a rough finger cup. With full replacement sliders,
verify smooth travel, free return and switch release before and after closing the
lid, in the mounted orientation. Existing contacts and stops are unchanged.

Record printer, material, nozzle, layer height, support/bridge settings and the
chosen depth against revision 016. Physical fit and comfort status: **pending**.
