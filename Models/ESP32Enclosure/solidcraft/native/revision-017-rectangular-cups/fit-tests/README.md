# Full rectangular spherical cups: quick feel print

Print [finger-cup-samples.3mf](finger-cup-samples.3mf). These are nominal 3 mm
upper sections extracted from the actual native slider states, with the real
rectangular outline, chamfer and spherical surface. They print **cup-side up**
on a flat cropped base. [Preview](finger-cup-samples.png).

| Left to right along print-layout X | Nominal center depression |
| --- | --- |
| `cup_050_up` | 0.5 mm |
| `cup_060_up` | 0.6 mm, default |
| `cup_080_up` | 0.8 mm |

Depth is measured from the former flat face. The sphere/chamfer also lowers the
corners slightly, so total sample height is slightly less than the original
3 mm cap. Each piece is independently seated on the bed. The dish spans the
entire rectangular top; it has no circular inset boundary or flat top border.

Use your intended slider material/nozzle/layer settings. These small cup-up caps
have flat bases and need no supports. Confirm that in your slicer. They test feel
and surface finish, not guide fit, travel, strength, or support removal from a
complete slider. Compare the three depths under a fingertip and record which
feels best, plus printer, material, nozzle, layer height and observations.

For a working test, print the [two replacement sliders](../replacement-sliders.3mf).
Those are also cup-side up but need slicer support review under their raised
arms/contact bosses and rear lugs. Protect guide/magnet/insert fits. Reassemble,
adjust the nylon contact tips, and check smooth full travel, free return and
switch release before and after closing the lid in the mounted orientation.
